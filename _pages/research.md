## Dissertation

**Securing The Skies: Safety-Constrained Decentralized Multi-UAV Coordination With Deep Reinforcement Learning**  
Ph.D. Dissertation, University of New Mexico, 2024.

My doctoral work studied how decentralized multi-agent systems can learn coordinated behavior while respecting safety and temporal-spatial constraints. The research sits at the seam between control theory and reinforcement learning: policy learning supplies adaptability, while explicit safety structure keeps the learned behavior connected to physical constraints and real-world deployment needs.
